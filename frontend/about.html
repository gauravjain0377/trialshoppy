🤖 AI Processing Pipeline
1

Image Validation & Preprocessing

Uploaded photo is validated (format, size), resized to a standard resolution, and normalized for landmark detection.

Pillow · OpenCV
2

Body/Face Landmark Detection

For clothing: MediaPipe Pose detects 33 body landmarks (shoulders, hips, wrists). For eyewear: MediaPipe Face Mesh detects 468 face points to locate eye positions precisely.

MediaPipe Pose · MediaPipe Face Mesh
3

Product Background Removal

The product image has its background automatically removed using rembg's U2Net model, producing a clean RGBA cutout with transparent background.

rembg · U2Net Deep Learning Model
4

Perspective Transform & Warping

Using the detected landmarks, OpenCV applies perspective warping to fit the product to the body's shape, angle, and scale.

OpenCV · getPerspectiveTransform · warpPerspective
5

Alpha Blending & Compositing

The warped product is composited onto the user's photo using alpha blending, preserving natural lighting and creating a realistic overlay.

OpenCV · PIL · Alpha Compositing

⚠️ Known Limitations

  • Best results with front-facing, well-lit photos. Side angles reduce landmark detection accuracy.
  • Clothing overlay works best for upper-body garments. Full-body outfits require additional model tuning.
  • The try-on is a realistic overlay simulation — not photorealistic diffusion-based generation (which requires GPU).
  • Product images are emoji/flat representations in demo mode. Real product images improve results significantly.
  • Processing speed depends on host machine's CPU. GPU acceleration would dramatically improve speed.
